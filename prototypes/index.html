Traffic lights and the Prism logo alone in one top row. The whole sidebar drops below, navigation starts at OVERVIEW. Brand without the model router control caption.

Traffic lights alone in the top zone, sidebar below carrying the full brand and caption inside. The smallest edit to the current layout: just add a top inset, moving nothing.

One full-width titlebar: traffic lights, logo, and sync status in a single hairline-bordered strip. Brand-free sidebar starting at OVERVIEW. Classic desktop look.

Sidebar shrinks to icons with tooltips, logo in the rail mark at the top. Maximum room for content, sections Ov / Op / Cf / Sy. More radical than the rest, and it radically fixes the tightness.

Traffic lights, a sidebar collapse button, and the logo in the titleline. Clicking the button smoothly hides the rail and the content takes the width. Try it, it is live.

Sidebar and logo as in production today: sidebar under the traffic lights, logo in the titleline. Logs filters are lifted into the window header, right of the logo: search and segments live in the titleline, the page keeps only its heading and refresh.
